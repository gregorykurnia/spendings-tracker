# B: Daybook (one home, no tab bar)
- **Idea:** opening the app shows what is waiting. Every extracted line is a row with its own tick; one bar accepts all that look right. Repeats and incoming money are pulled into their own group.
- **Moving around:** hub and spoke. Home is the inbox. One quiet line says where you stand this month and opens Spending. All transactions and Categories are rows below the list (a side column on desktop). The thumb zone at the bottom holds the daily action, not navigation.
- **Density:** balanced. Hairline rows, no cards; the open row shows the note and category close by.
- **Type:** Hanken Grotesk, heavy weights for the count and amounts, tabular figures.
- **Colour:** lemon-white ground, petrol blue ink and action, lemon marks the group that needs a look. Category colours carry the rest.
- **Name and mark (provisional):** Daybook, the book where each day's spends were written. A bookmarked page.
