# A: Kas (one at a time)
- **Idea:** checking is a short conversation. One transaction fills the screen with one big Accept; the rest wait behind it.
- **Moving around:** a floating three-item dock (Check with its count, Spending, History). Categories sit behind History. On desktop the dock moves to the top and an "Up next" list sits beside the card.
- **Density:** low. One amount at 60 to 76px, everything else close by it. The two items that need a look sit last in the queue.
- **Type:** Plus Jakarta Sans (drawn for Jakarta), tabular figures for rupiah.
- **Colour:** peach milk ground, white card, aubergine ink, plum for the one action, apricot for what needs a look.
- **Name and mark (provisional):** Kas, Indonesian for cash book. Two overlapping coins.
