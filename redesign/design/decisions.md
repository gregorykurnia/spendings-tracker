# Decisions

## Proposals (open: not adopted until the owner answers)

**P1. Merge "accept one" and "accept all" (R3, R7) into one Accept concept with a scope.**
Same action, same word; the scope is one line or everything that looks right. All three directions already show it this way. Adopt?

**P2. Merge R4, R9 and R10 into one "needs a look" concept.**
Throwing something away, a likely repeat and money coming in are three reasons a line isn't a normal spend. One concept means one place, one reason line in plain words and the same two ways out: throw it away, or keep it. Direction B groups them; A puts them last in the queue; C flags them in place. Adopt?

**P3. Treat R6 (corrections kept if interrupted) as a rule, not a feature.**
Nothing to see or tap: edits to a waiting line are saved as they happen. It stays in the design notes as behaviour. Adopt?

**P4. One form for R2, F6 and C2.**
Correcting a waiting line, changing a saved one and typing one by hand use the same fields (date, store, amount, category, note). Design it once and reuse it. Adopt?

## Choices made in stage 1 (for the record)
- The mock batch is ordered so the five clean lines come first in A and B; C keeps the bank's own order.
- Store names are shown exactly as the bank writes them (capitals, stars, codes). Everything else is sentence case.
- The screenshot in C is a generic bank list with no bank branding.
